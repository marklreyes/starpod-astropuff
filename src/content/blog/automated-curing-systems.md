Curing is a crucial step in cultivation that takes place after drying. This stage allows residual moisture and chlorophyll to gradually dissipate while terpenes fully develop, enhancing the final product’s aroma, potency, and overall quality¹. While drying and curing are often mentioned together, they serve different purposes²—curing stabilizes the flower, making it more refined and enjoyable.

For small-scale growers, mason jars are the traditional go-to. However, this method requires constant attention³, with daily burping multiple times per jar. The manual nature of this process and the physical constraints of jar storage make it impractical for those scaling up production.

## Why Consider an Automated Curing System?

Derek Gilman’s Automated Curing System offers a streamlined alternative that eliminates the hassle of manual burping. If your harvest exceeds two pounds of flower, this system becomes an efficient solution, replacing the labor-intensive mason jar method.

Gilman’s system utilizes 5-gallon buckets equipped with gamma-sealed lids that feature rubber gaskets, ensuring an airtight environment. Each bucket accommodates approximately 920 grams of flower. The setup includes two strategically placed holes: one at the bottom for intake and another at the top for air escape. These are connected to check valves and a perforated silicone hose, allowing for even airflow. An air pump, controlled by an automated timer, pressurizes the bucket daily to release excess gases—a fully automated burping process that maintains a consistent curing environment.

## The Benefits of an Automated Curing System

* **Time Efficiency:** Manual burping is labor-intensive and time-consuming. Automation frees up valuable hours, allowing growers to focus on other critical aspects of cultivation.
* **Consistency & Quality Control:** This system ensures a uniform curing environment, reducing human error and the risk of contamination. Environmental factors such as humidity, pressure, and airflow are better managed, leading to a more predictable and refined product.
* **Adaptability:** Different strains require varying curing times⁴. An automated system allows growers to tailor settings based on the specific needs of each varietal, ensuring optimal results.

## Scaling Up: Is It Worth the Investment?

One of the standout advantages of this automated approach is scalability. Once the system is set up, additional buckets can be integrated seamlessly, adjusting to yield fluctuations. However, there are a few considerations:

* **Upfront Costs:** A 5-gallon bucket with a gamma lid costs between $20-$50 per unit⁵. In Gilman’s demonstration, 36 buckets were used, totaling a potential investment of $1,800.
* **Storage Space:** Finding room for multiple buckets is a logistical challenge. A dedicated curing space with controlled climate conditions is essential, as seen in Gilman’s setup, which included an HVAC-equipped, windowless room.

## Traditional vs. Automated: What’s the Verdict?

The key difference between traditional mason jar curing and an automated system is the level of human involvement. With jars, every container requires individual attention, increasing the risk of uneven airflow, contamination from handling, and inconsistent results. Moreover, improper timing can lead to over-drying, which degrades flower quality through oxidation.

Automating the curing process eliminates these concerns. It standardizes airflow, reduces handling, and minimizes human error—all while saving labor costs that can be reallocated to other areas of the operation.

## Bonus Tip: The Power of Two-Way Humidipacks

For an extra layer of control, two-way humidipacks are a game-changer. These packs regulate moisture levels by adding or removing humidity as needed⁶, ensuring optimal storage conditions. Best of all, they require no maintenance—just drop one into each container and let it do the work.

## Final Thoughts: Is Automation Right for You?

If you’re a hobbyist with a modest yield, mason jars remain a cost-effective and functional solution. However, for commercial cultivators handling larger harvests, an automated curing system is a game-changer. It provides efficiency, consistency, and scalability, making it an invaluable investment for those looking to optimize their post-harvest process.

Whether you’re curing a few ounces or managing a large-scale operation, refining your approach ensures that your flower reaches its highest potential—because when it comes to cultivation, quality is everything.
